ISMS02 · framework

Removed in ISMS02 - Roles and Responsibilities · approved by Sarah Waters on 2026-09-11.

Reason: Oxford’s published document has no Information Governance Framework section (or its Top Management / governance-body subsections); the register shows this as a deletion rather than silently missing content.

Baseline text removed:

The Information Governance Framework

The high-level structure of the ISMS’s Information Governance Framework is represented in the diagram below.

Top Management

The ISMS Top Management consists of University of Oxford’s Senior Information Risk Owner and the OxTRE Programme Board, chaired by the Senior Information Risk Owner. The Senior Information Risk Owner is a member of University of Oxford’s executive and reports to the Council, providing a direct reporting line from the ISMS to University of Oxford’s primary body responsible for operational management, which oversees institutional strategy, risk management, and key policies. The Senior Information Risk Owner receives assurance that relevant processes, procedures and policies are in place to protect University of Oxford’s information assets. The OxTRE Programme Board receives reports from the Operational Management Group, holds it to account, and in turn provides advice and assurance to the Senior Information Risk Owner.

Full details of the OxTRE Programme Board’s membership and responsibilities are set out in its terms of reference.

Full details of University of Oxford’s formal committees are published on its governance pages.

Operational Management Group

The Operational Management Group reports to the OxTRE Programme Board to assure that the ISMS is operating effectively, delivering information security objectives and managing information risks within tolerance. While the Operational Management Group is responsible for ISMS policies, it may escalate risks to the OxTRE Programme Board that cannot be resolved directly. Risk decisions are therefore ultimately owned by the OxTRE Programme Board.

The Operational Management Group is composed of members of the ISMS’s governance, technical, and service teams. Full details of membership and responsibilities of individual roles are set out in its terms of reference.

The Trusted Research Environments (as defined in the classification and tiering policy) that form part of the scope are managed by Research Computing. They are represented at the Operational Management Group to ensure these environments are operating safely.

Privileged Access Management

Administrative or privileged accounts shall be tightly controlled and limited to authorized personnel. Privileged accounts should only be used for administrative activities. Privileged access management shall be controlled through a formal process and only the minimum privileges will be granted to carry out the role or task. A formal record of all privileges allocated will be maintained.

ISMS03 · os-access

Replaced in ISMS03 - Access Control Policy · approved by Sarah Waters on 2026-09-11.

Reason: Oxford’s Operating System Access bullets differ in wording throughout (each starts ‘All users shall be required to…’) and name the University of Oxford Password Policy explicitly.

Local text:

Operating System Access
  • All users shall be required to confirm they are an authorised user at log-on.
  • All users shall be required to change their passwords periodically and in accordance with the University of Oxford Password Policy.
  • All user sessions shall be configured to lock automatically after a period of inactivity in order to reduce the risk of unauthorised access.
  • Where technically possible, all standard accounts that are delivered with operating systems shall be disabled, deleted or have their “default” passwords changed on system installation.

Replaced in ISMS03 - Access Control Policy · approved by Sarah Waters on 2026-09-11.

Reason: Oxford’s Monitoring and Logging paragraph differs in wording throughout (log field list, ‘anomalous activity’, and conditional reporting on a judgement that unauthorised access occurred) and drops the baseline’s reference to organisation-specific incident management procedures.

Local text:

Monitoring and Logging

Access to Trusted Research Environments shall be logged and monitored for the purpose of investigating security incidents. Logs should include ‘date/time/name/reason’ and should be reviewed periodically to detect any unauthorised access attempts. Anomalous activity or violations shall be assessed and, where unauthorised access is judged to have occurred, will be reported as an information security incident.
